Companionship Chatbot

Listen for the tone, not just the words.

Category
CONVERSATIONAL AI
Project
03 / 06

In 30 seconds

Problem
Make an LLM companion listen for the tone of a message, not just its words.
My part
I built an LLM companionship chatbot with sentiment analysis.
Result
Sentiment classification reached 95% accuracy, and measured user satisfaction was 20% higher.
Proof
  • 1st Place, KSU CDay 2024
  • See the evaluation lab: a public reproduction on open data, not the original model

One limit

Neither the original model nor its data is published here, so the 95% and the 20% can’t be checked on this site.

The evaluation lab shows how I evaluate a classifier instead, on open data: a held-out test set, bootstrap intervals, a threshold you can move, and failures picked by rule, not by hand. Its models learned from review sentences. Chat messages, sarcasm and mixed feelings are harder, and these models have never seen them.
