# Original artwork

Production SVG artwork lives in `web/assets/`, except the two inline drawings in `web/index.html` (hero orbit accent, introduction signal path). No images from the Instagram reel or private career package are shipped.

- `about-flow.svg`: original deterministic vector flow field, authored for this site on November 8, 2026. Streamlines curve around several vortices; pale contour rings and seed points create a monochrome illustrated sky. Inspired by the reference's swirling contrast and density, without copying its painting or silhouette.
- `beam-figure.svg`: original vector figure, authored for this site on November 10, 2026. A 190 x 270 transparent silhouette of a person drifting head-first and slightly turned, built from tapered limb strokes (white near side, `#cfd1dc` far side); it is generated geometry, not traced from the reference or any photo. Shown once, by `<img class="beam-figure">` inside `#introduction .cinematic-art` (`web/index.html`, decorative: `alt=""`, the drawing is `aria-hidden`). The glow is CSS, not part of the file: `.beam-figure` in `web/about-scenes.css` applies `grayscale(1) brightness(1.2)` and three white `drop-shadow`s, rotates the figure against the beam (`--fig-tilt`, `--beam-turn`), and pins its head (ellipse centre 39.5% / 18.9% of the box) to the start of the signal path with `--head-ax: .395`, `--head-ay: .189`, `--fig-ratio: 1.421` (height / width) and `transform-origin: 39.5% 18.9%`. Size per breakpoint is `--fig-w`.
  - To swap in another figure (for example a cutout photo): export it with a transparent background (PNG or WebP, about 2x the largest `--fig-w`, so 220 px wide or more; or an SVG), replace the `src`, `width` and `height` of the `<img>`, and set `--fig-ratio` to its height / width and `--head-ax` / `--head-ay` (plus `transform-origin`) to the head centre as fractions of the image box. The CSS filter turns any image into a white glowing silhouette; drop `grayscale(1) brightness(1.2)` to keep a photo's own tones. Check that the head still meets the trail, at 1440, 1280, 768 and 390 px wide, and update `tests/about-scenes.test.js`, which checks those numbers against the SVG's head ellipse.
- Signal path (inline SVG in `#introduction .cinematic-art`, `web/index.html`): original vector art, authored for this site on November 10, 2026; it replaced `light-sculpture.svg` (abstract porcelain loops from November 8, which read as a letter and was removed). One faint luminous line tells the scene's heading: it leaves the falling figure's head as a jagged, multi-lead EEG-like trace (four faint leads, pinched together at the head, converging into one), settles into a calm signal down the beam, and resolves into a small graph of six agent nodes, the brightest being the orchestrator. Monochrome strokes with a Gaussian-blur bloom and a dark umbra; the waveform is generated deterministically (seeded noise, spike-and-wave shapes), not traced from any recording. It draws itself on scroll (`web/about-scenes.css`) and is fully drawn without scroll-driven animation support, with reduced motion, and without JavaScript.
- `grain.svg`: original SVG turbulence texture for the cinematic panel.
- The hero's orbit accent (inline SVG in `web/index.html`; the former `sculpture.svg` file was removed on November 10, 2026), project-cover illustrations and favicon were authored for the initial draft. Project covers are representative illustrations, not production screenshots.

The scene compositions and scroll movement still need desktop/mobile browser comparison against reference checkpoints. The portrait, exact typeface, final figure (the original `beam-figure.svg` can be swapped as described above) and full nine-scene fidelity remain open in SPECIFICATION.md.

- License texts come from `github.com/google/fonts` (`ofl/archivo/OFL.txt`, `ofl/geistmono/OFL.txt`) and ship next to the font files.
- Both files are the latin subset only (`unicode-range` U+0000-00FF plus common punctuation, U+2191 and U+2193). Characters outside it, such as the ↗ ↘ ← → ↔ arrows, render from the local fallback fonts.
- `Archivo Fallback` and `Geist Mono Fallback` are local Arial/Helvetica and Courier New/Menlo faces with `size-adjust` and ascent/descent overrides measured in Chrome against these files, to limit layout shift during the swap.
- Geist Mono is unmodified (Google's latin subsetting only). Archivo's axes were narrowed to what the site uses: display 850 at 87.5% width, body 400–700 at 100% (CSS weights 400–850; nothing below 400). Google's CSS2 API serves the same full-axis latin file for narrower ranges, so the limit was applied locally; brotli for woff2 came from Node's built-in zlib through a temporary shim. Headlines were compared at 1440 against the original file: identical glyph extents and layout, differences only in edge anti-aliasing. Any new weight below 400 or width outside 87.5–100% would be synthesized or clamped, so widen the instance first. OFL permits bundling and redistribution with the site; neither copyright line declares a Reserved Font Name. Keep the license files beside the fonts.
