# Original artwork

Production SVG artwork lives in `web/assets/`, except the inline hero orbit accent in `web/index.html`. No images from the Instagram reel or private career package are shipped.

- `about-flow.svg`: original deterministic vector flow field, authored for this site on November 8, 2026. Streamlines curve around several vortices; pale contour rings and seed points create a monochrome illustrated sky. Inspired by the reference's swirling contrast and density, without copying its painting or silhouette.
- `beam-figure.svg`: original vector figure, authored for this site on November 10, 2026 (redrawn the same day; it replaced a head-first falling figure). A 200 x 320 transparent silhouette of a person ascending: upright, head tipped back so the face and chest turn up, arms raised and open, legs together and trailing with pointed feet, fading slightly toward the toes. Tapered limb outlines around hand-placed centrelines (white near side, `#cfd1dc` far side) plus a torso outline and a head ellipse; generated geometry, not traced from the reference, Cesar or any photo. Shown once, by `<img class="beam-figure">` inside `#introduction .cinematic-art` (`web/index.html`; decorative: `alt=""`, the art is `aria-hidden`, `pointer-events: none`). The glow is CSS, not part of the file: `.beam-figure` in `web/about-scenes.css` applies `grayscale(1) brightness(1.2)` and three white `drop-shadow`s and leans the figure into the shaft by `--fig-tilt` (12deg).
  - Placement: `.cinematic-art` is the figure's own box, `--fig-w` wide (per breakpoint) and `--fig-ratio` (height / width, 1.6) tall. The image point `--body-ax` / `--body-ay` (.5 / .5, the box centre) sits `--fig-x` across the stage and `--art-bias` (.56) of the way down the open area between the topline (`--art-top`) and the copy (`--foot-top`, also the copy's top margin), so the figure can't reach the text. `--head-ax` / `--head-ay` (.505 / .113, the head ellipse centre) place the halo.
  - To swap in a cutout photo of Cesar: export it upright with a transparent background (PNG or WebP, about 2x the largest `--fig-w`, so 340 px wide or more), replace the `src`, `width` and `height` of the `<img>`, and set `--fig-ratio` to its height / width, `--body-ax` / `--body-ay` to the point that should sit at the scene's centre (usually the chest or hips), and `--head-ax` / `--head-ay` to the head centre, all as fractions of the image box. The filter turns any image into a glowing white silhouette; drop `grayscale(1) brightness(1.2)` to keep a photo's own tones. Check the figure at 1280, 1440, 1728, 768 and 390 px wide, and update `tests/about-scenes.test.js`, which checks those numbers against the SVG's head ellipse and size.
- Ascending light (CSS only, `web/about-scenes.css`): a soft halo (`.beam-halo`, a radial gradient stretched along the shaft), ten small light motes (`.beam-motes > span`, a strip turned onto the shaft), and a brighter shaft core toward the top-right end (`.cinematic-light::after`, masked to grow up the shaft). Monochrome, no new colour. On scroll (`--cine-scene` view timeline) the figure rises up the shaft with a slight sway, the halo swells, the motes drift up at different speeds and the upper light grows. Without scroll-driven animation support, with reduced motion, without JavaScript and in print, everything rests in place, with the figure centred and fully visible. It replaced the inline EEG-and-agent-graph signal path (November 10, 2026), which itself had replaced `light-sculpture.svg` (abstract porcelain loops from November 8).
- `grain.svg`: original SVG turbulence texture for the cinematic panel.
- The hero's orbit accent (inline SVG in `web/index.html`; the former `sculpture.svg` file was removed on November 10, 2026), project-cover illustrations and favicon were authored for the initial draft. Project covers are representative illustrations, not production screenshots.

The scene compositions and scroll movement still need desktop/mobile browser comparison against reference checkpoints. The portrait, exact typeface, final figure (the original `beam-figure.svg` can be swapped as described above) and full nine-scene fidelity remain open in SPECIFICATION.md.

- License texts come from `github.com/google/fonts` (`ofl/archivo/OFL.txt`, `ofl/geistmono/OFL.txt`) and ship next to the font files.
- Both files are the latin subset only (`unicode-range` U+0000-00FF plus common punctuation, U+2191 and U+2193). Characters outside it, such as the ↗ ↘ ← → ↔ arrows, render from the local fallback fonts.
- `Archivo Fallback` and `Geist Mono Fallback` are local Arial/Helvetica and Courier New/Menlo faces with `size-adjust` and ascent/descent overrides measured in Chrome against these files, to limit layout shift during the swap.
- Geist Mono is unmodified (Google's latin subsetting only). Archivo's axes were narrowed to what the site uses: display 850 at 87.5% width, body 400–700 at 100% (CSS weights 400–850; nothing below 400). Google's CSS2 API serves the same full-axis latin file for narrower ranges, so the limit was applied locally; brotli for woff2 came from Node's built-in zlib through a temporary shim. Headlines were compared at 1440 against the original file: identical glyph extents and layout, differences only in edge anti-aliasing. Any new weight below 400 or width outside 87.5–100% would be synthesized or clamped, so widen the instance first. OFL permits bundling and redistribution with the site; neither copyright line declares a Reserved Font Name. Keep the license files beside the fonts.
